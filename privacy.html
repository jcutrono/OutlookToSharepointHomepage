This policy explains how FileMy.Email handles personal data across two things: our website at filemy.email, and the Outlook add-in ("the Add-in") that saves your emails and attachments to SharePoint or OneDrive. The two are very different in what they touch, so we describe them separately below.

Part B — The Outlook Add-in

The Add-in files emails and attachments into your organization's own SharePoint or OneDrive. To do that, our service, hosted on Microsoft Azure in the United States, reads each message from Microsoft 365 and writes it to the folder you chose. This section explains exactly what the service reads, what it keeps, and for how long.

What the Add-in Accesses

The Add-in acts as the signed-in user through Microsoft Graph, with the permissions your organization approves, so it can reach only what that user can already reach. When a message is filed, the service reads:

  • Sender and recipient names and email addresses
  • Subject line and date
  • Attachment file names
  • Message identifiers
  • The message body and attachments, so they can be copied to your SharePoint or OneDrive

The metadata is also used to suggest a destination folder, name the saved file and fill in any SharePoint columns you map. If you turn on Auto-Archive, the service also reads the sender and other metadata of each new message in your Inbox to check it against your filing rules. Messages that match no rule are not copied or stored. To help you find folders, the service can also list the names and web addresses of SharePoint sites in your organization, and it offers you only the sites you can open.

How Your Email Is Filed

When a message is filed, our service downloads it and its attachments from Microsoft 365 and uploads them to the SharePoint or OneDrive folder you chose, over encrypted connections. The content is held in memory only for that transfer. It is never written to our database and is not kept afterwards. If you save emails as PDF, the conversion runs on our service, which loads any remote images the email refers to, as an email client would. The Add-in does not share your email content with any analytics provider or advertising network, and it adds nothing to the emails you send.

What the Add-in Stores

Our database, in Microsoft Azure's East US region, holds:

  • Filing rules: sender or recipient addresses or domains, and the SharePoint or OneDrive folder each one maps to. Folder names are yours and may include client or project names.
  • Filing history, used to prevent duplicate saves: message identifiers, the saved file name (by default the date, sender name and subject), the folder, a link to the file, and attachment file names.
  • Save on Send requests: the subject, recipient addresses and chosen folder, from when you click Send until the message is filed.
  • Settings: file-naming templates, SharePoint column mappings and other preferences.
  • Encrypted Microsoft sign-in tokens, so Save on Send and Auto-Archive can work when the Add-in is closed. The encryption keys are held separately in Azure Key Vault.
  • Subscription record: your organization's Microsoft 365 tenant ID, which users hold licenses, and the billing contact's email address.

Our service also keeps logs for 30 days. Diagnostic logs contain user and tenant identifiers, message identifiers and error details, and for each filed message its subject, sender address and destination folder. Access logs record each database request and each read of a stored secret, with the identity that made it.

What the Add-in Does NOT Store

  • Message bodies or attachment contents
  • Your password
  • Copies of any files saved to SharePoint or OneDrive

Authentication

The Add-in uses your organization's existing Microsoft 365 sign-in (OAuth 2.0). We never see or store your password. Your administrator can revoke the Add-in's access at any time in Microsoft Entra, which also stops Save on Send and Auto-Archive.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run FileMy.Email:

  • Microsoft — Microsoft Azure hosts the Add-in's service, its database, logs and encryption keys in the East US region, and sends our internal alert emails. The Add-in reads from and files into your own Microsoft 365 tenant. (privacy statement)
  • Google — website analytics (privacy policy)
  • Stripe — payment processing (privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare — DNS for our domains and delivery of our website. Add-in traffic does not pass through Cloudflare. (privacy policy)
  • GitHub — hosting for our website (privacy statement)

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

International Data Transfers

We are based in the United States, and the Add-in's service and data are hosted in Microsoft Azure's East US region. Our service providers (including Microsoft, Google, Stripe, Cloudflare and GitHub) process data in the United States and other countries. If you are outside the United States, including in the UK, the EEA or Canada, your personal data will be transferred to the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU–US and UK Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum / IDTA) put in place by these providers.

Data Retention

We keep website analytics data for up to 26 months, payment records for as long as required for tax and accounting law, and support correspondence for as long as needed to assist you.

Add-in data is kept while your organization's subscription is active. We delete it within 30 days after the subscription is cancelled or we receive a verified deletion request, apart from the payment records above. When an administrator removes a user's seat, they can choose to delete that user's data at the same time. The service's logs are deleted automatically after 30 days, and database backups after 8 hours. Files you have saved stay in your SharePoint or OneDrive and are not affected.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (TLS 1.2 or later) and is encrypted at rest. The Add-in's database accepts only named Microsoft Entra identities, with key-based access switched off. Our service and our deployment pipeline each have their own identity with only the permissions they need, and every database request and every read of a stored secret is logged with the identity that made it. The Add-in relies on Microsoft 365 OAuth and never stores your password. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
