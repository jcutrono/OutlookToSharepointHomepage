Stop dragging emails into folders by hand

If your team's process for saving Outlook email to SharePoint is "drag the message to a desktop, rename the file, upload it through the browser, then go back and tag the metadata columns" — you're losing minutes per email and people quietly stop doing it. Saved emails end up scattered between someone's PST, a OneDrive, and a SharePoint library that's missing half the thread.

This add-in turns the whole flow into one click, inside the message you're already reading. It learns the folder you use for each contact, fills SharePoint metadata columns from the email headers, and handles attachments without a second upload step. Outgoing mail is covered by Save on Send — pick a destination while composing, then forget about it.

How it works

1

Open the email

The add-in opens in the side panel. It reads the sender and subject, then surfaces a smart folder suggestion based on past saves.

2

Pick a SharePoint folder

Keep the suggested folder ticked, click a Recent folder, or browse and search any SharePoint site you can access.

3

Press File email

The email and its attachments upload directly to SharePoint. Metadata columns are populated. The add-in remembers the choice for next time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save an Outlook email to SharePoint?

Install the add-in from the Microsoft Office store, open any email, pick a folder in the side panel (or accept the smart suggestion), and press File email. The email and its attachments upload directly to SharePoint with metadata columns filled in.

Can I save sent email to SharePoint automatically?

Yes — turn on Save on Send while composing and pick a destination folder. The email files itself seconds after it sends.

What format are emails saved in?

EML (preserves the full email including headers — Outlook's native format) or PDF (universally readable, good for archives and audits). Set a default in settings, override per save.

Does it work in Outlook on the web and desktop?

Yes. The add-in runs in Outlook desktop, Outlook on the web, and the new Outlook for Windows. Folder rules and learned suggestions sync across all of them.

Do my emails or attachments pass through your servers?

Only in memory, for the moment it takes to file them. Our service reads each message from Microsoft 365 with your credentials and writes it straight into your SharePoint or OneDrive. Email content and attachments are never stored on our servers.

Can I use it with shared and delegate mailboxes?

Yes, from the FileMy.Email pane. Open the email in a shared mailbox, or in a mailbox you have delegate access to, and save it, its attachments or its whole thread to SharePoint or OneDrive. Inbox Cleanup and Auto-Archive cover only your own mailbox. It works in Outlook on the web, the new Outlook for Windows, classic Outlook for Windows (version 2304 or later) and Outlook for Mac, but not in Outlook for iOS or Android. A Microsoft 365 admin approves one extra permission the first time anyone opens FileMy.Email on a message in a shared or delegate mailbox.
